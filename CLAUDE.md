## Scenario 3 decisions so far

**Role framing:** "Imagine you are a Business Development and Product Leader within the Schneider Electric Services division."

**Nine pillars:** EcoConsult, EcoCare, EcoFit, Digital Services, Field Services, Spare Parts, Cybersecurity, Sustainability, Energy Advisory. ("Energy Advisory" was transcribed from speech as "energy at wise"; confirm if unsure.)

**Today's core pillars, ranked:** 1 EcoCare, 2 EcoFit, 3 Field Services, 4 Digital Services, 5 Spare Parts, 6 EcoConsult, 7 Cybersecurity.

**Future growth potential, ranked:** 1 Digital Services, 2 EcoCare, 3 Sustainability and Advisory, 4 EcoFit, 5 Cybersecurity.

**Strategies:** A Defend the Core · B Scale Digital Services · C Data Center, Energy Landscape and AI Transformation (Chen's replacement for "Lead with Sustainability").

**Chosen bet:** Strategy B, used to make the core harder to commoditize. Data centers and AI are pursued through Digital Services, partnerships and greenfield projects, not a standalone consulting practice.

**Winning aspiration:** Be our customers' trusted, go-to service partner for their three biggest challenges: maximizing uptime, preventing unplanned downtime, and improving energy efficiency. We defend the core (EcoCare and EcoFit across our installed base) and, building on it, become known as one of the best digital service providers, combining maintenance, advisory, and AI-driven predictions to optimize all three.

**Hard no:** We will not compete on price for routine, time-based maintenance against independent service providers.

**Objective:** Become our customers' go-to digital service provider and most trusted partner, making a name for Schneider in the new energy landscape for data centers and AI-related segments, while our core pillars, led by EcoCare, remain the foundation that funds and supports this growth.

**Key results (three years):**
- **KR1:** Digital Services revenue (led by digital twin and AR) grows from a 10M baseline at 11% CAGR to **13.68M by Year 3**, becoming a top-three revenue line in Services. Path: 11.10M, 12.32M, 13.68M (10 × 1.11³ = 13.676; +3.68M, +36.8%).
- **KR2:** At least **5M incremental revenue** from data center and AI segments, via new partnerships or greenfield projects, by Year 3.
- **KR3:** EcoCare and EcoFit grow **3% organic + 5% leveraged** (read as per year), and service contract satisfaction rises **+10 points** over today's score.
